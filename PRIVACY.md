# Elec Calc Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Elec Calc does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

- The app works entirely on your device. Calculations are not sent anywhere.
- The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.
- The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- Values you type are kept only while the app is open and are not saved.

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the date above will change.
