Grounding Electrode Conductor

Table 250.66 · 250.66(A)–(C)

    Main / Supply-Side Bonding Jumper

    Table 250.102(C)(1) · 250.28(D) · 250.102(C)
    • Use the equivalent area of all parallel conductors in one phase (250.102(C)(1)).
    • In parallel raceways, size each raceway's jumper from the conductors in that raceway (250.102(C)(2)).
    • Load-side equipment bonding jumpers are sized by the OCPD on the EGC tab (250.102(D)).

    Equipment Grounding Conductor

    Table 250.122 · 250.122(B) upsizing
    • EGC never has to be larger than the circuit conductors (250.122(A)).
    • Parallel raceways: full-size EGC in each raceway, based on the OCPD (250.122(F)).
    • Upsizing applies when ungrounded conductors are increased for anything other than meeting ampacity (voltage drop, etc.).

    Ground Rod Resistance Estimate

    250.53(A)(2) · 250.53(B) · Dwight formula
    • A single rod needs a supplemental electrode unless it measures 25 Ω or less (250.53(A)(2) Exc.).
    • Rods must be at least 6 ft apart; spacing equal to rod length or more works better.
    • Typical soil: wet loam ~3,000 Ω·cm, clay 5,000–10,000, sand/gravel 50,000+.
    • This is an estimate for planning. Measure with a fall-of-potential or clamp tester to verify.

            Voltage Drop
            210.19(A) Info. Note · 215.2(A) Info. Note · 647.4(D) · 695.7 · Ch. 9 Table 8 · 250.122(B)
            
  
            
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
            
  
            
  
            
  
              
    
            • Formula: VD = k × K × L × I ÷ cmil, with k = 2 (1φ) or 1.732 (3φ), K = 12.9 Ω·cmil/ft copper, 21.2 aluminum (Ch. 9 Table 8, 75 °C).
              • 
    
            • The NEC recommends no more than 3% on a branch circuit or feeder and 5% total (Informational Notes, not enforceable unless locally adopted). Fire pumps (695.7) and sensitive electronics (647.4(D)) are mandatory limits.
              • 
    
            • For 1/0 and larger on AC circuits at low power factor, reactance adds to the drop. Use Ch. 9 Table 9 effective Z for those.
              • 
    
            • When conductors are upsized for voltage drop, the wire-type EGC must be upsized by the same area ratio (250.122(B)).

            • The NEC only fixes colors for the grounded conductor (white, gray, or 3 white stripes, 200.6), the EGC (green, green with yellow stripes, or bare, 250.119), and the high leg (orange, 110.15). Phase colors are industry convention.
              • 
    
            • Where a building has more than one system voltage, each ungrounded conductor must be identified by phase and system, and the method posted at each panelboard (210.5(C)(1), 215.12(C)(1)). Keep the same scheme throughout the building.
              •

              •

              • 
    
            • A white conductor in a cable may be re-marked as ungrounded, e.g. a switch loop, with tape or paint at every point it's visible (200.7(C)). Never re-mark a green conductor as anything else.
              • 
    
            • Phase arrangement at panels and switchboards: A, B, C from front to back, top to bottom, or left to right (408.3(E)). High leg on B, except in meter sockets, where it goes on C.
